Method

Equation (1): tensile stress area
As = π/4 (d − 0.9382p)²
π/4 (10.0 − 0.9382 × 1.5)² = 57.99 mm²

As: tensile stress area; d: nominal diameter; p: pitch.

Equation (2): proof load
Fp = SpAs
640 MPa × 57.99 mm² = 37.11 kN

Fp: proof load; Sp: proof strength.

Equation (3): target preload
Fi = αFp
0.75 × 37.11 kN = 27.83 kN

Fi: target preload; α: proof-load fraction.

Equation (4): tightening torque
T = KdFi
0.15 × 10.0 mm × 27.83 kN = 41.8 N·m

T: tightening torque; K: nut factor.

Show intermediate steps
  1. Resolve d and p from the selected ISO thread.
  2. Calculate As, then multiply it by Sp.
  3. Apply α to the proof load.
  4. Use the selected K to estimate tightening torque.

Background

Assumptions, validity, and references

This model gives an early torque target. A joint verification also needs tightening scatter, stiffness, external load, embedding, fatigue, thread stripping, bearing pressure, and operating environment.
