Force values and exact attachment points

Coordinates use the ground frame: x forward, y left, z normal. The section uses u inward from the selected edge and z normal. The reactions are the resultants required for equilibrium; an outside reaction cannot be supplied by these contacts.

Library research and what each approach borrows

The closest mechanics-specific options are Meclib and PrairieLearn’s drawing components. JSXGraph and Three.js are running in studies F and E. The other six studies are original renderers using the same calculated positions and forces.

Library / referenceUseful ideasFit for this tool
MeclibMechanics symbols, interactive FBDs, supports and force objects built on JSXGraph.Closest specialist reference. Its STACK/Moodle workflow needs adaptation. Reuse permission was not established from the repository overview; no code copied.
PrairieLearn pl-drawingExplicit arrow anchor at head or tail, vector labels, force and moment drawing tools.Good drawing conventions. Built on Fabric.js and tied to PrairieLearn. Current client-side source is under AGPL terms; no source copied.
PrairieDrawDrawing in physical coordinates, transformations, arrows and engineering annotations.A model for B’s Canvas approach. PrairieLearn recommends pl-drawing for new questions.
JSXGraphLinked points, arrows, automatic label positioning, measurements, pan and zoom. SVG output.Version 1.13.3 under MIT. F uses fixed geometry; the separate interaction lab tests constrained dragging, dependent geometry, and automatic labels.
Three.js ArrowHelperExplicit 3D origin, direction and length. Orbit controls expose hidden attachments.Working trial in E, version 0.180.0 under MIT. Adds a camera and WebGL; keep a 2D view for reading moments.
KonvaCanvas arrows, selectable objects and hit testing.Useful if this becomes a diagram editor. It does not supply the force model or label placement; no runtime trial here.

My starting preference is A for the main calculation, D for explaining moments, and E as an optional inspection view. B makes slope easier to read. C separates forces that share a point, G gives labels their own space, and H connects the section to the footprint. F compares rendering and navigation; the separate interaction lab tests JSXGraph’s draggable geometry.
