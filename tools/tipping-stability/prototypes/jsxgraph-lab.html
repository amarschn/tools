Force values and calculation notes

100 kg assembly, 1.2 m wheelbase, 0.8 m track, and G at 0.6 m height on level ground. A moves above the footprint center. The outline is illustrative. Positive moments restore about the selected left edge. Reactions are the resultants required for equilibrium; an outside reaction cannot be supplied by these contacts.

Fᵤ: force inward from the edge; Fz: upward force; u, z: application coordinates. The crowded case adds 0.8 m/s² acceleration toward +u and a 160 N force directed 175° from +u at G. This experiment does not check sliding or individual contact loads.

What this tests in JSXGraph

The round handle is a point constrained to a segment; the square handle is constrained to a circle. Dependent points keep the arrow attached. An orthogonal projection constructs the moment arm, and a measurement label follows its length. Toggle automatic label placement and try the crowded case to inspect overlaps and label movement.

Geometry responds during dragging. Python recalculates forces and reactions after a short pause; pending reactions and moments are hidden until the current calculation finishes. The existing stability solver remains the calculation source. This page reuses the gallery’s JSXGraph runtime and loads the same Pyodide version as the calculator.
