# Data license

The original curated and generated data in this repository is available under
the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Individual observations retain source-specific attribution and provenance in
[`curated/sources.json`](curated/sources.json). Public-domain and
manufacturer-published source material is cited rather than relicensed here.
Downstream users are responsible for following the terms attached to each
source.

The records marked `prototype-seed-estimates-v0` are explicitly non-production
demonstration data and must not be used for engineering design.
