# Engineering Standards Automation Candidates

Date: 2026-07-15

| Field                        | Standard or code           | Typical design task                                                                                                     | Why it is tedious                                                                                                                                                                              | Automation potential                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bolted joints                | **VDI 2230**               | Size a highly loaded bolted joint and determine preload, clamp load, bolt stress, fatigue margin, and tightening torque | Many sequential calculation steps involving joint stiffness, load introduction, preload scatter, embedment loss, friction, eccentricity, and fatigue                                           | **Excellent**                                                                   |
| Gears                        | **ISO 6336 / AGMA 2101**   | Rate spur and helical gears for tooth-root bending and surface pitting                                                  | Large number of geometry, material, load-distribution, dynamic, life, reliability, and application factors                                                                                     | **Excellent, but established software exists**                                  |
| Bearings                     | **ISO 281**                | Calculate bearing life                                                                                                  | Basic calculation is easy, but modified life adds reliability, lubrication, contamination, fatigue-limit, and load-spectrum factors                                                            | **Good, but heavily commoditized**                                              |
| Lifting fixtures             | **ASME BTH-1**             | Design lifting beams, spreader bars, lifting lugs, clamps, and below-the-hook devices                                   | Requires selection of design category and service class, followed by strength, buckling, fatigue, weld, pin, bearing, and connection checks                                                    | **Excellent niche**                                                             |
| Pressure vessels             | **ASME BPVC Section VIII** | Determine shell/head thickness, nozzle reinforcement, MAWP, external-pressure capacity, and test pressure               | Extensive material tables, allowable stresses, joint efficiencies, geometry rules, charts, exemptions, and fabrication-dependent requirements                                                  | **Very high value, very high complexity**                                       |
| Process piping               | **ASME B31.3**             | Size pipe wall, check branch reinforcement, determine allowable stress and test pressure                                | Material tables, temperature-dependent properties, corrosion allowance, weld factors, pressure design equations, component ratings, branch rules, and exceptions                               | **Excellent as narrowly scoped modules**                                        |
| Pressure relief              | **API 520 / API 521**      | Size relief valves and depressuring systems                                                                             | Designer must determine the governing scenario and then select gas, liquid, steam, two-phase, fire, backpressure, and correction-factor methods                                                | **Excellent**                                                                   |
| Building electrical          | **NFPA 70 / NEC**          | Size conductors, overcurrent devices, grounding conductors, raceways, boxes, and motor circuits                         | Interacting ampacity tables, terminal-temperature limits, ambient corrections, conductor-count derating, OCPD exceptions, motor rules, conduit fill, box fill, and grounding rules             | **Excellent, but edition and jurisdiction handling are difficult**              |
| Industrial control panels    | **UL 508A Supplement SB**  | Determine panel short-circuit current rating                                                                            | Requires tracking every power-circuit component, its SCCR, permitted protective-device combinations, feeder limitations, transformers, current-limiting devices, and tested combinations       | **One of the best targets**                                                     |
| Arc flash                    | **IEEE 1584**              | Calculate incident energy and arc-flash boundary                                                                        | Complex mathematical models combined with fault-current studies, protective-device clearing times, electrode configuration, enclosure dimensions, and system operating modes                   | **High value, dominated by major power-system packages**                        |
| HVAC ventilation             | **ASHRAE 62.1**            | Calculate required outdoor air for zones and air systems                                                                | Occupancy classification, people and area rates, diversity, distribution effectiveness, system ventilation efficiency, recirculation, exhaust, and multi-zone calculations                     | **Excellent scoped calculator**                                                 |
| Residential HVAC             | **ACCA Manual J, S and D** | Calculate loads, select equipment, and size ducts                                                                       | Detailed envelope inputs, orientation and fenestration data, load factors, OEM performance interpolation, duct fitting equivalent lengths, pressure budgets, and balancing                     | **Excellent workflow, but licensing and approved-software requirements matter** |
| Commercial energy compliance | **ASHRAE 90.1**            | Verify envelope, HVAC, lighting, controls, and energy compliance                                                        | Climate-zone lookups, equipment thresholds, economizer requirements, fan-power calculations, heat recovery, insulation tables, control sequences, exceptions, and alternative compliance paths | **Excellent rule-engine problem**                                               |
| Fire sprinklers              | **NFPA 13**                | Lay out and hydraulically calculate sprinkler systems                                                                   | Hazard classification, spacing rules, obstruction rules, design-area selection, density, K-factors, hose allowance, and iterative pipe-network calculations                                    | **High value, mature software market**                                          |
| Structural loads             | **ASCE 7**                 | Calculate wind, seismic, snow, rain, flood, and load combinations                                                       | Site hazard data, risk category, exposure, topography, enclosure classification, coefficients, irregularities, and many conditional procedures                                                 | **Excellent, but hazard lookup is already partially automated**                 |
